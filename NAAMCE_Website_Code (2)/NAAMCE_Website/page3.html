Our project story

For this class project, we imagined three students who met during an aviation design challenge. One loved technology, one loved connecting people, and one loved turning complex ideas into clear designs.

Their fictional venture began with a shared goal: make advanced air mobility more approachable. Inspired by NAAMCE’s name, they created this website concept to bring learning experiences and curious people together.

This is a fictional origin story for the assignment, not the founding history of the real organization.

Frequently asked questions

A few things before takeoff.

Do I need aviation experience?

In our fictional program, beginners are welcome. Curiosity is the only prerequisite.

Can I fly an aircraft during Discovery Day?

No flight is included in this project scenario. The experience is an introduction to aviation concepts.

Is this the official NAAMCE website?

No. This is a student-created concept. The services, pricing, founder story, and contact details are fictional.

Can I actually book a service here?

No. The catalog and inquiry form demonstrate how a business website could work; no booking or payment is processed.
